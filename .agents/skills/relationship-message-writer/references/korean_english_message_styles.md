# Korean and English Message Styles

## Korean: calm sincere style

Good for daily care, apology-adjacent moments, and non-flashy surprises.

- “오늘은 당신이 조금 덜 애썼으면 해서 준비했어.”
- “부담 갖지 말고 그냥 편하게 받아줘.”
- “내가 더 자주 표현해야 하는데, 오늘은 말보다 행동으로 해보고 싶었어.”

## Korean: playful style

Good for Pepero Day, small desserts, casual date.

- “이건 그냥 당신 생각나서 산 거야. 빼빼로데이 핑계도 살짝 있고.”
- “오늘은 내가 데이트 코스 담당이야. 마음에 안 들면 바로 플랜 B로 갈게.”

## English: warm style

- “No big occasion. I just wanted you to feel looked after today.”
- “I found this because it reminded me of you, and I wanted to make the day a little softer.”
- “You do so much for us. I wanted to take care of this one small thing.”

## Avoid

- “You said I never do surprises, so here.”
- “I spent a lot, so you should like it.”
- “This is what everyone likes these days.”
- “This tool told me to do this.”
