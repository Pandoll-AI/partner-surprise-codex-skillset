# Travel Burden Rubric

Score from 1 to 5. Lower burden is usually better for a surprise.

| Factor | 1 low burden | 5 high burden |
|---|---|---|
| Travel time | under 30 min | over 3 hours |
| Packing | none | multi-day family packing |
| Schedule | already free | requires rearrangement |
| Childcare | already covered | unresolved |
| Cost risk | cancellable | prepaid/non-refundable |
| Energy | restorative | crowded/complex |

## Good surprise travel pattern

- The user handles logistics.
- Partner can decline or modify without guilt.
- The reveal includes a simple reason and a clear option to choose timing.
- There is a home-based backup.
