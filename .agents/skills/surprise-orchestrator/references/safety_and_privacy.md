# Relationship Safety and Privacy Guardrails

## Allowed

- Using dates, preferences, interests, allergies, budget, shared routines, and openly provided context.
- Checking public restaurant, travel, gift, event, and trend information.
- Suggesting honest pretexts such as “요즘 여기가 평이 좋아서 같이 가보고 싶었어” when verified.
- Suggesting low-pressure gestures after a hard day.

## Not allowed

- Reading partner's private emails/messages/photos without consent.
- Location tracking or covert monitoring.
- Fake excuses, fake reviews, fake celebrity claims, or fabricated scarcity.
- Emotional pressure: “I did all this, so you should be happy.”
- High-cost commitments without clear budget and cancellation path.

## Preferred framing

Use:

- “문득 생각나서 준비했어.”
- “요즘 당신이 좀 힘들어 보여서 부담 없는 걸로 골라봤어.”
- “특별한 날이 아니어도 챙기고 싶었어.”
- “여기가 요즘 평이 좋아서 같이 가보고 싶었어.” only if verified.

Avoid:

- “네가 저번에 불만이라고 해서 했어.”
- “도구가 추천해서 했어.” unless it becomes a playful aside after the moment.
- “이 정도 했으니까 됐지?”
