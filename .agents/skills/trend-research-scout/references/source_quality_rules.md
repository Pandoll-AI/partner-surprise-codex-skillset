# Source Quality Rules for Surprise Trend Research

## Do

- Cite recent, relevant sources when live browsing is available.
- Separate factual venue details from subjective popularity claims.
- Check dates. A “new opening” from two years ago is no longer new.
- Confirm price, delivery, booking, holiday closure, and cancellation policies close to execution.
- Search in Korean for Korean venues/gifts and in English for international sources.

## Do not

- Treat one influencer post as proof.
- Call a place “Michelin”, “celebrity favourite”, “viral”, or “sold out” without evidence.
- Use outdated listicles as current trend evidence.
- Hide uncertainty.

## Confidence scoring

- **High**: official source plus one current independent corroborating source, or official current booking/product page.
- **Medium**: multiple recent independent mentions, but official details are incomplete.
- **Low**: weak trend signal from social/blog only.
- **Unknown**: browsing unavailable or no reliable source found.
