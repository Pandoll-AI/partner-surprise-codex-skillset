# Korea-context Romantic and Care Calendar

Use this as a stable baseline. Some dates are commercial or unofficial; only recommend them if partner profile suggests the partner enjoys seasonal rituals.

| Date | Occasion | Usefulness |
|---|---|---|
| 14 Feb | Valentine's Day | Useful if the partner likes classic couple rituals or chocolate/dessert |
| 14 Mar | White Day | Useful in Korea/Japan-influenced couple culture; small gift/date |
| 14 May | Rose Day | Optional; flowers or garden/cafe date |
| 8 May | Parents' Day Korea | Useful for recognising their family/caregiving role |
| 11 Nov | Pepero Day | Light, playful, low-cost gesture |
| 24–25 Dec | Christmas Eve/Christmas | Dinner, hotel, performance, family plan |
| 31 Dec–1 Jan | Year-end/New Year | Reflection letter, short trip, gratitude ritual |

Lunar holidays such as Seollal and Chuseok vary yearly. Do not hard-code them unless the date is provided or verified for the year.
