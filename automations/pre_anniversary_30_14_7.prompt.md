# Pre-anniversary staged planning automation

Use `$life-event-trigger-radar` and `$surprise-orchestrator`.

Task:

Check whether a high-importance anniversary, birthday, or family milestone is 30, 14, or 7 days away. If yes, create a staged plan.

Rules:

- 30 days out: brainstorm and reserve cancellable options.
- 14 days out: finalise gift/restaurant/travel choice and logistics.
- 7 days out: confirm booking, delivery, childcare, message/card.
- Do not leave premium ideas to last-minute execution.

Output:

- Date and occasion
- What stage we are in
- Decisions needed
- Today’s action checklist
- Failure modes and backups
