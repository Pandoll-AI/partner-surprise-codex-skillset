# Monthly trend watch automation

Use `$trend-research-scout`.

Task:

Find partner-friendly trends in Seoul/Korea for the next month across:

- restaurants/cafes
- hotel staycations
- exhibitions/performances
- gifts/brands
- seasonal travel or flowers

Rules:

- Use current sources where possible.
- Separate verified facts from weak trend signals.
- Do not recommend anything unless it can become a natural, honest reason for a surprise.

Output:

For each category:

1. Trend idea
2. Evidence status
3. Why it might fit my partner
4. Search/source notes
5. Potential surprise angle
