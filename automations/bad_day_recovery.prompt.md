# Bad-day recovery gesture prompt

Use `$relationship-message-writer`, `$gift-idea-selector`, and `$surprise-orchestrator`.

Task:

Create a low-pressure recovery gesture for my partner after a stressful or disappointing day.

Rules:

- The goal is comfort, not fixing or controlling their mood.
- Avoid expensive or dramatic gestures unless the partner explicitly likes them.
- Prefer practical care + small personal detail + short sincere message.
- Make it easy for the partner to decline conversation and rest.

Output:

1. One action under 30 minutes
2. One optional small item
3. Exact Korean message
4. What not to say
5. Plan B if they want space
